Extract zero or more explicitly reported human melanoma regression cases.
Keep separate patients as separate case objects.

Rules:
- Extract only explicitly supported information.
- Do not infer missing clinical facts.
- NOT_REPORTED is preferable to guessing.
- Distinguish fact from author interpretation sentence by sentence.
- Section name is context only; it does not determine Evidence type.
- Documented patient observations in Discussion are OBSERVED_FACT.
- Language such as may, might, likely, suggest, postulate, or could is
  AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION.
- Do not infer causality.
- Preserve uncertainty and conflicts.
- Every REPORTED or REPORTED_ABSENT field must cite contiguous source text.
- Copy quotes verbatim. Never join passages with "..." or an ellipsis.
- Regression after an event does not prove the event caused regression.
- primary_site uses REPORTED_ABSENT with null value and evidence when the
  primary site was investigated but not identified. Do not use NOT_REPORTED
  for an explicit "no primary identified" finding.
- regression_extent_clinical is COMPLETE, PARTIAL, UNCERTAIN, or NOT_REPORTED.
  Do not infer COMPLETE clinical regression from pathology saying no viable
  tumor cells.
- viable_tumor_at_pathology is PRESENT, ABSENT, NOT_ASSESSED, or UNCERTAIN.
  Use ABSENT when pathology explicitly reports no viable tumor cells.
- Keep regression_start_date, first_observed_reduction,
  regression_confirmed_date, and regression_duration separate.
- regression_start_date is REPORTED only when the source explicitly states
  regression onset/beginning or gives an exact onset date. A title or phrase
  such as "following biopsy" expresses sequence, not an onset date.
- A duration such as "throughout 19 months" is regression_duration or
  follow-up evidence, not regression_start_date.
- Preserve relative wording; never invent a calendar date.
- treatment_status is only one of: no_treatment, active_treatment,
  prior_treatment, treatment_stopped, treatment_completed, unknown.
- Surgery, biopsy, radiation sessions, drug administrations, imaging, and
  hospitalization are Events, not treatment_status values.
- treatment_before_regression contains oncologic treatment history. If its
  timing relative to regression is unclear, use UNCERTAIN.
- If one observation is repeated in Abstract, Case Report, Figure Caption, or
  Discussion, return one field value with multiple Evidence references.
