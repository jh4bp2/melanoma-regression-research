Extract a chronological clinical timeline only for the described patient case.

Rules:
- Extract only explicitly supported information.
- Do not infer missing clinical facts or calendar dates.
- NOT_REPORTED is preferable to guessing.
- Distinguish fact from author interpretation.
- Do not infer causality.
- Preserve uncertainty.
- Every event must cite exact source text and page.
- Copy relative phrases such as "two weeks before regression" verbatim into
  relative_time and use RELATIVE precision.
- Regression after an event does not prove the event caused regression.
- Discussion mechanisms are AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION, not OBSERVED_FACT.
- A Discussion sentence that directly restates a patient measurement, imaging
  result, pathology result, treatment, or clinical event may be OBSERVED_FACT.
- Classify may/might/suggest/speculate/hypothesize/possible mechanism/likely
  due to/could represent/probably related language as AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION.
- Surgery, biopsy, radiation, drug administration, imaging, hospitalization,
  infection, fever, progression, and regression are Events when documented.
- Never convert an unclear treatment time into a calendar date or inferred
  before/after relation.
- Each source quote must be one contiguous verbatim span. Never join passages
  with "..." or an ellipsis; use separate Evidence references.
