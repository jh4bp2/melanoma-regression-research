Extract only directly observed biological findings for the specified melanoma case.
This task creates an observation layer, not a mechanism or hypothesis layer.

Core rules:
- Do not infer biological mechanisms, causes, pathways, or treatment effects.
- Do not convert author hypotheses into BiologicalObservation objects.
- Association, sequence, and co-occurrence do not imply causation.
- Preserve the source variable wording in variable_name.
- normalized_variable is additional; never replace the source wording with it.
- Preserve explicit absence as REPORTED_ABSENT with direction ABSENT.
- A variable not mentioned in the paper is NOT_REPORTED, not absent.
- Return only mentioned findings in observations. Missing target variables are
  calculated separately by the system; do not manufacture NOT_REPORTED quotes.
- Preserve numeric source values, units, initial/follow-up values, and timing.
- Do not create a direction unless the source explicitly reports a state or change.
- Preserve relation to regression and temporal precision without inventing dates.
- Every observation requires one or more contiguous verbatim source quotes.
- Never join non-contiguous text with an ellipsis.
- Section name does not determine Evidence type.
- A documented patient finding in Discussion is an observed fact.
- Statements using may, might, likely, suggest, postulate, could, hypothesize,
  believe, or similar mechanistic language are author interpretations.
- Put relevant author interpretations in rejected_interpretations with evidence;
  never store them as observations.
- If Abstract, Case Report, Figure Caption, or Discussion repeats one finding,
  return one canonical observation with multiple Evidence references.

Target categories and variables:
- IMMUNE: CD8 T cells, CD4 T cells, NK cells, Treg, macrophages, dendritic
  cells, lymphocytes, immune infiltration.
- IMMUNE checkpoints: PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4 (category IMMUNE).
- INFLAMMATORY: IFN-alpha, IFN-beta, IFN-gamma, IL-2, IL-6, TNF-alpha, CRP,
  fever, systemic inflammation.
- METABOLIC: glucose, FDG uptake, SUV, lactate, iron, oxygen, hypoxia, lipid
  metabolism.
- TUMOR_MICROENVIRONMENT: immune infiltration, angiogenesis, tumor perfusion.
- PATHOLOGIC: tumor necrosis, viable tumor cells, apoptosis, fibrosis,
  lymphocytic infiltration, melanin pigment.
- ENDOCRINE: cortisol, catecholamines, hormonal changes.
- MICROBIOME: infection, identified organism, microbiome change.
- VASCULAR, NEURAL, GENETIC, and OTHER directly observed findings.

Numeric changes:
- A directly reported pair such as SUV 3.4 followed by SUV 0.9 may be one
  DECREASED observation.
- Keep the source expression in value and a machine-readable representation in
  normalized_value when possible.
- FDG/SUV reduction is an observed metabolic imaging change. It is not proof of
  a glucose-metabolism mechanism.

Pathology:
- Store tumor necrosis and viable tumor status as separate observations.
- "No viable tumor cells" is REPORTED_ABSENT / ABSENT for viable tumor cells.
- Necrosis does not prove immune-mediated killing.

Case and timeline context supplied by the system may be used only to identify
the patient and temporal relation. All observation values still require
verbatim evidence from SOURCE TEXT.
