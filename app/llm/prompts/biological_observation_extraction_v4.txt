Extract only directly observed biological and disease findings for the specified
melanoma case. This is an observation ontology task, not mechanism inference.

The source text may be tagged:
- [SHARED_BOTH] applies to every patient in the paper and may be used
- [CASE_SCOPE_UNCERTAIN] must not be assigned as a unique fact for this patient
- Do not use sentences about a different named patient (Patient A vs Patient B).

General rules:
- Do not infer mechanisms, causes, pathways, or treatment effects.
- Do not convert author hypotheses into observations.
- Put may/might/likely/suggest/postulate/could/believe/hypothesize statements
  in rejected_interpretations with exact evidence.
- Preserve source variable wording, source values, numbers, units, timing,
  explicit absence, and uncertainty.
- Every observation requires contiguous verbatim evidence. Do not join distant
  sentences or invent ellipsis quotes.
- A finding documented in Discussion remains OBSERVED_FACT if it is observed.
- Split mixed sentences: the observed clause is an observation; the
  mechanistic clause is a rejected interpretation.
- Repeated findings across sections become one observation with multiple
  Evidence references.
- Missing variables are calculated by the system; do not invent evidence.

observation_domain:
- BIOLOGICAL_STATE: measured host/tumor biological state, including CBC,
  lesion-specific FDG/SUV, tumor necrosis, viable tumor status, and
  immune-cell infiltration even when obtained from pathology.
- DISEASE_PHENOTYPE: lesion size/morphology, progression, recurrence,
  spontaneous regression, and metastatic phenotype. Clinical disappearance
  belongs here.
- DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE: biopsy diagnosis, immunohistochemistry used only
  for diagnosis, staging evidence, and diagnostic stains.
- CLINICAL_CONTEXT: symptoms, infection, fever, vaccination, leukoderma,
  and immune-related adverse events (irAEs). irAE is context, not a cause
  of regression.
- TREATMENT_RESPONSE: only a directly reported response or resistance to
  a named treatment. Spontaneous regression is never TREATMENT_RESPONSE.

domain_secondary:
- Use when one finding has two semantic roles.
- CD3/CD8/lymphocyte infiltration: domain_primary BIOLOGICAL_STATE,
  domain_secondary DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE, measurement_semantics
  PATHOLOGIC_FINDING, category IMMUNE.

measurement_semantics:
- DIRECT_MEASUREMENT, IMAGING_PROXY, PATHOLOGIC_FINDING, LAB_MEASUREMENT,
  MORPHOLOGIC_FINDING, SYMPTOM, CLINICAL_FINDING, or UNKNOWN.
- FDG uptake and SUV are IMAGING_PROXY.
- Histopathology is PATHOLOGIC_FINDING.
- Leukoderma is CLINICAL_FINDING, category DERMATOLOGIC_PHENOTYPE.

Lesion identity:
- Use laterality + anatomical region + lesion type
  (right inguinal lymph node, left axillary lymph node,
  right heel primary melanoma lesion).
- Do not emit names such as "right lesion", "skin lesion",
  or "lymph node lymph node".
- Do not collapse multifocal, bilateral, in-transit, or multi-station
  lymph-node sets into one lesion. Use lesion_collection_identifier.
- Do not invent unnamed member lesions for a collection.
- Primary disappearance and persistent nodal metastasis are compatible
  lesion states in one case. Do not treat them as a contradiction.

Split these even if they share one sentence:
- no viable malignant cells
- fibrosis
- melanophages
- leukoderma
- lymphocytic infiltration
- necrosis

Direction:
- A single static value or range has direction UNKNOWN.
- "decreased from 15 mm to 8 mm" may be DECREASED.

Genotype:
- Not limited to BRAF/NRAS/KIT/NF1. Include MEFV and cytogenetic findings
  such as del(5q).
- "BRAF V600E wild type" is gene BRAF, variant V600E, state WILD_TYPE.
  Never invert this to MUTATED.
- "MEFV P369S/R408Q germline variant" is VARIANT_PRESENT, origin GERMLINE.
  Preserve both protein changes. Do not reduce it to ABSENT/PRESENT.
- "mutation not detected" is NOT_DETECTED, not wild-type.

Regression:
- Multiple spontaneous regression episodes in one patient stay separate
  by organ and time.
- Delayed possible ipilimumab, vaccine, infection, pyrin, or biopsy
  explanations go in rejected_interpretations, not observations.

Association and Event linkage never imply causation.
