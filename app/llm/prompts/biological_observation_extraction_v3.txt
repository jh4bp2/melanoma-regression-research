Extract only directly observed biological and disease findings for the specified
melanoma case. This is an observation ontology task, not mechanism inference.

General rules:
- Do not infer mechanisms, causes, pathways, or treatment effects.
- Do not convert author hypotheses into observations.
- Put may/might/likely/suggest/postulate/could/believe/hypothesize statements
  in rejected_interpretations with exact evidence.
- Preserve source variable wording, source values, numbers, units, timing,
  explicit absence, and uncertainty.
- Every observation requires contiguous verbatim evidence.
- A finding documented in Discussion remains OBSERVED_FACT.
- Split mixed sentences: the observed clause is an observation; the
  mechanistic clause is a rejected interpretation.
- Repeated findings across sections become one observation with multiple
  Evidence references.
- Missing variables are calculated by the system; do not invent evidence.

observation_domain:
- BIOLOGICAL_STATE: measured host/tumor biological state, including CBC,
  lesion-specific FDG/SUV, tumor necrosis, viable tumor status, and
  immune-cell infiltration even when obtained from pathology.
- DISEASE_PHENOTYPE: lesion size/morphology, progression, recurrence,
  spontaneous regression, and metastatic phenotype.
- DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE: biopsy diagnosis, immunohistochemistry used only
  for diagnosis, staging evidence, and diagnostic stains.
- CLINICAL_CONTEXT: symptoms, infection, postoperative infection, fever,
  and contextual clinical findings. If an infection Event exists, also
  emit a CLINICAL_CONTEXT observation.
- TREATMENT_RESPONSE: only a directly reported response or resistance to
  a named treatment. Spontaneous regression is never TREATMENT_RESPONSE.

domain_secondary:
- Use when one finding has two semantic roles.
- CD3/CD8/lymphocyte infiltration: domain_primary BIOLOGICAL_STATE,
  domain_secondary DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE, measurement_semantics
  PATHOLOGIC_FINDING, category IMMUNE.

measurement_semantics:
- DIRECT_MEASUREMENT, IMAGING_PROXY, PATHOLOGIC_FINDING, LAB_MEASUREMENT,
  MORPHOLOGIC_FINDING, SYMPTOM, CLINICAL_FINDING, or UNKNOWN.
- FDG uptake and SUV are IMAGING_PROXY, not proof of a metabolic mechanism.
- Histopathology is PATHOLOGIC_FINDING.
- CBC and other blood tests are LAB_MEASUREMENT.

Lesion identity:
- Preserve a stable anatomical lesion_identifier.
- Use the same identifier for aliases of one lesion
  (left lower lobe nodule / previously biopsied left lung nodule).
- Do not collapse multifocal, bilateral, or in-transit sets into one lesion.
- Set lesion_scope_kind to SINGLE_LESION, LESION_COLLECTION,
  MULTIFOCAL_DISEASE, or UNKNOWN.
- Use lesion_collection_identifier for multifocal/in-transit/bilateral sets.

Direction:
- Direction requires an explicit same-measurement baseline comparison.
- "lesions measuring 5-15 mm" or "ranged from 1 mm to 10 mm" has
  direction UNKNOWN.
- "decreased from 15 mm to 8 mm" may be DECREASED.
- A single time-point SUV or normal CBC has direction UNKNOWN.

Genotype:
- BRAF wild-type is a reported WILD_TYPE state, not ABSENT.
- "mutation not detected" is NOT_DETECTED, not the same as wild-type.
- Do not store genotype as REPORTED_ABSENT.

Temporal fields:
- Preserve temporal_text such as "approximately 2 years later",
  "6 weeks after surgery", "during the following 19 months".
- Fill temporal_value, temporal_unit, temporal_relation, and
  temporal_precision when explicit. Never invent a calendar date.

Regression:
- Spontaneous regression / complete disappearance without a named
  treatment response belongs to DISEASE_PHENOTYPE.
- Delayed possible ipilimumab effect is not TREATMENT_RESPONSE.
  Put that claim in rejected_interpretations.

Association and Event linkage never imply causation.
