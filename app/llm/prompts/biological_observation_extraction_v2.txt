Extract only directly observed biological and disease findings for the specified
melanoma case. This is an observation ontology task, not mechanism inference.

General rules:
- Do not infer mechanisms, causes, pathways, or treatment effects.
- Do not convert author hypotheses into observations.
- Put may/might/likely/suggest/postulate/could/believe statements in
  rejected_interpretations with exact evidence.
- Preserve source variable wording, source values, numbers, units, timing,
  explicit absence, and uncertainty.
- Every observation requires contiguous verbatim evidence.
- A finding documented in Discussion remains OBSERVED_FACT.
- Repeated findings across sections become one observation with multiple
  Evidence references.
- Missing variables are calculated by the system; do not invent evidence.

observation_domain:
- BIOLOGICAL_STATE: measured host/tumor biological state, including CBC,
  lesion-specific FDG/SUV, tumor necrosis, and viable tumor status.
- DISEASE_PHENOTYPE: lesion size/morphology, progression, recurrence, disease
  course, and metastatic phenotype.
- DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE: biopsy diagnosis, immunohistochemistry, staging evidence,
  primary-site investigation, and diagnostic stains.
- CLINICAL_CONTEXT: symptoms and contextual clinical findings.
- TREATMENT_RESPONSE: directly reported response or resistance to treatment.

measurement_semantics:
- DIRECT_MEASUREMENT, IMAGING_PROXY, PATHOLOGIC_FINDING, LAB_MEASUREMENT,
  MORPHOLOGIC_FINDING, SYMPTOM, CLINICAL_FINDING, or UNKNOWN.
- FDG uptake and SUV are IMAGING_PROXY, not proof of a metabolic mechanism.
- Histopathology is PATHOLOGIC_FINDING.
- CBC and other blood tests are LAB_MEASUREMENT.

Lesion scope:
- Use PATIENT, SYSTEMIC, LESION, LYMPH_NODE, or OTHER.
- Preserve a stable anatomical lesion_identifier whenever lesion-level.
- Use REGRESSING_TARGET, PROGRESSING_NON_TARGET, STABLE_NON_TARGET, or UNKNOWN.
- Never combine findings from different lesions into one observation.
- A known lesion role requires a lesion identifier.

Behnia lesion separation:
- The biopsied left lower lobe lesion is the REGRESSING_TARGET.
  Its size decrease and minimal FDG uptake/SUV 2.5 remain separate findings.
- Bilateral upper-lobe lesions are PROGRESSING_NON_TARGET.
  Their increased size/number and SUV 15.2 remain separate from the target.

Direction and qualitative level:
- Direction is INCREASED, DECREASED, PRESENT, ABSENT, UNCHANGED, MIXED, UNKNOWN.
- A single time-point measurement must not be labeled changed without an
  explicit same-lesion, same-measurement baseline comparison.
- Minimal FDG uptake or SUV 2.5 alone has direction UNKNOWN and
  qualitative_level MINIMAL.
- Markedly hypermetabolic SUV 15.2 alone has direction UNKNOWN and
  qualitative_level MARKED.
- A normal CBC has value NORMAL, direction UNKNOWN, qualitative_level NORMAL.
- Use LOW, MINIMAL, NORMAL, HIGH, MARKED, or UNKNOWN without converting level
  into change direction.

Ong validation:
- Preserve target-lesion SUV 3.4 to 0.9 as a DECREASED BIOLOGICAL_STATE
  observation with explicit numeric comparison.
- Preserve tumor necrosis as PRESENT and viable tumor cells as
  REPORTED_ABSENT/ABSENT.
- Classify diagnosis, staging, and follow-up findings in their proper non-
  biological domains.

Association and Event linkage never imply causation.
